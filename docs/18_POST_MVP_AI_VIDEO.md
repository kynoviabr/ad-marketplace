# FASE 13 — AI Concierge / WhatsApp Lead Qualification
Optional paid portal-provided AI assistant for advertisers. It handles initial inquiries, answers advertiser-configured FAQs, filters low-intent inquiries and hands qualified leads to the professional.

Flow: contact -> virtual assistant -> FAQ/qualification -> lead status -> human handoff.

The assistant must be transparently identified as a virtual assistant, not deceptively impersonate the professional.

Candidate capabilities: configured FAQs, service region, configured availability, configured price/range information where permitted, inquiry intent/time, lead qualification, human handoff, concise lead summary and conversation/handoff analytics.

Guardrails: do not invent availability/pricing, take unauthorized financial decisions, receive service payments on the professional's behalf or retain unnecessary sensitive data. Age/risk signals require appropriate safe handling.

Potential monetization: premium plan or paid add-on. Exact pricing remains an experiment.

Future funnel: contact_click -> ai_conversation -> qualified_lead -> human_handoff.
Candidate evolved North Star: Qualified Leads per Active Advertiser.

# FASE 14 — Video Profiles
