# Product Requirements
STATUS: APPROVED
VERSION: 1.0
LAST UPDATED: 2026-08-15

Classified advertising portal for verified adult independent professionals. Advertisers manage profiles; visitors browse and use external contact methods. The platform does not intermediate subsequent communication or transactions.

## Monetization
Founder, Essential, Premium, Top, Super Top, boosts and sponsored visibility.
Founder launch period may be promotional; current planned minimum Founder price is R$99.99/month and must be configurable.

## MVP
Auth, KYC/age verification, public profile, locations, filters, photos, moderation, billing, founder rules, boosts, basic analytics, SEO and audit logs.

## Out of MVP
Internal chat, visitor-to-advertiser payments, escrow, commissions, native apps, video/streaming, recommendation AI, microservices, multi-country complexity.

## Commercial launch objective
OBJ-001 — Target R$50,000 in monthly portal revenue by the end of month 6 after commercial launch. This is an operating target, not a forecast or guarantee.

## Geographic rollout
GEO-001 — Initial commercial launch and first six-month growth phase focus exclusively on São Paulo capital.
GEO-002 — Rio de Janeiro is the planned second market after the São Paulo phase.
Architecture remains multi-city capable, but launch operations and acquisition prioritize marketplace density in São Paulo.

## Early acquisition model
Use a hybrid acquisition strategy:
- direct 1:1 advertiser acquisition/onboarding
- digital lead generation
- referrals/community
- SEO/demand generation

A Community & Onboarding role may be used for direct acquisition. No identity documents should be collected over WhatsApp or informal channels; KYC stays inside the secure verification flow.

Measure: outreach -> response -> interest -> signup -> KYC -> approved profile -> active profile -> paid conversion.
